Pediophobia

Interactive Installation

Many people have something they are afraid of—spiders, hospitals, heights, or dolls. For me, it has always been dolls. This kind of fear is often overlooked, and rarely do people consider how it might be addressed.

While studying education, I learned about exposure therapy, a method that gradually reduces fear through controlled and repeated confrontation. I initially explored this idea through more common fears, but without personally feeling that fear, it was difficult to evaluate the effectiveness of the design.

Eventually, I returned to my own fear. From looking at images of dolls, to touching them, to designing and assembling my own models, and placing them throughout my living environment, I went through a continuous process of exposure. Through building this installation, I experienced a real process of desensitization.
