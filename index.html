About

I grew up spending time in hospitals where my mother worked. Later, through volunteer work in nursing homes and schools for children with autism, I began to realize that many groups in society are not easily heard or represented.

Inspired by Victor Papanek’s idea of designing for the real world, I believe design should respond to real human needs and bring visibility to overlooked experiences.
